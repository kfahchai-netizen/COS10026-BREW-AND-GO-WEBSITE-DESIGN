Member Benefits

Join our Brew Club today and enjoy exclusive perks that make your coffee experience even better. As a member, you'll unlock rewards, discounts, and special offers tailored just for you.

How to Join

Membership is free and easy! Sign up online through our website or app, or visit any of our locations to register at the counter. Once you're a member, start earning points on every purchase.

Earn Points

  • Earn 1 point for every RM1 spent on purchases.
  • Double points on Tuesdays for all members.
  • Bonus points for participating in events or referring friends.

Redeem Rewards

Use your points to redeem a variety of rewards:

  • 500 points: Free coffee
  • 1000 points: Free pastry or bakery item
  • 1500 points: RM10 off your next order

Exclusive Perks

Early Access

Get early access to new menu items, seasonal specials, and limited-edition drinks before they're available to the public.

Birthday Treat

Celebrate your birthday with us! Members receive a free drink or pastry on their special day.

Priority Ordering

Skip the line with priority ordering during peak hours. Place your order ahead and pick it up with ease.

Event Invitations

Be the first to know about exclusive events, tastings, and workshops. Members get priority invitations and discounts.

Stay Connected

As a member, you'll receive our monthly newsletter with tips, recipes, and insider updates. Opt-in to get the latest from Brew & Go right in your inbox.

Contact Us

Have questions about membership? Reach out to our team via the contact form or visit us in-store. We're here to help you make the most of your Brew Club experience!
