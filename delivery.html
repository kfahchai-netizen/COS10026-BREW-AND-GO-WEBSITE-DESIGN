Delivery Policy

At Brew & Go, we strive to deliver your favorite coffee and treats right to your doorstep with convenience and care. Please review our delivery policy below for all the details.

Delivery Areas

We currently offer delivery within a 5-mile radius of our main location at [Insert Address Here]. If you're outside this area, please visit us in-store or contact us for pickup options.

Delivery Fees

Delivery fees are calculated based on distance and order size:

  • Orders under RM50: RM15.99 delivery fee
  • Orders RM50-RM100: RM3.99 delivery fee
  • Orders over RM100: Free delivery

Fees may vary during peak hours or holidays.

Delivery Times

Our standard delivery window is 30-60 minutes from order placement. During busy periods (e.g., mornings or weekends), it may take up to 90 minutes. We aim to provide accurate ETAs via our app or website.

Minimum Order

There is no minimum order requirement for delivery, but please note that smaller orders may incur higher delivery fees.

Order Tracking

You can track your order in real-time through our app or website. We'll send updates on preparation and delivery status.

Special Instructions

If you have specific delivery instructions (e.g., leave at door, call upon arrival), please add them to your order notes. We appreciate any details to ensure a smooth delivery.

Cancellations and Refunds

Orders can be canceled within 10 minutes of placement for a full refund. Once prepared, cancellations may not be possible, but we'll work with you for future credits. Refunds for damaged or incorrect items will be processed promptly.
