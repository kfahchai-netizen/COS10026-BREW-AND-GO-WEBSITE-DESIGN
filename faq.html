Frequently Asked Questions

Welcome to Brew & Go! Get quick answers to our most common questions.

What is Brew & Go?

Brew & Go is your go-to spot for freshly brewed coffee, espresso drinks, and quick bites. We focus on quality ingredients and fast service for busy days.

What are your operating hours?

We're open Monday to Friday from 7 AM to 7 PM, and weekends from 8 AM to 6 PM. Check our website or app for any holiday closures.

Do you offer vegan or gluten-free options?

Yes! We have a variety of plant-based milks for lattes and gluten-free pastries. Our menu highlights these options clearly.

Can I order online or for delivery?

Absolutely! Use our website or app to place orders for pickup or delivery within a 5-mile radius. Delivery fees apply and vary by distance.

Do you have loyalty programs?

Yes, join our Brew Club for points on every purchase. Redeem them for free drinks or discounts. Sign up at the counter or online.

Is parking available?

We have limited street parking nearby. For larger groups, consider our drive-thru option for quick service.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, credit cards, and mobile payments like Apple Pay and Google Pay. No checks, please.
