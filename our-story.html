Our Story

About Brew & Go

Brew & Go was founded with a simple mission: to bring exceptional coffee experiences to the heart of the community. Established in 2023 by passionate coffee enthusiasts Daryl and Jennifer, our café has quickly become a beloved gathering spot in Kuching, Malaysia.

We source the finest beans from sustainable farms around the world and craft each cup with precision and care. Whether you're starting your day with a classic latte or exploring our artisan brews, Brew & Go is your go-to destination for quality coffee and warm hospitality.

Our commitment to excellence extends beyond our beverages—we're dedicated to creating memorable moments and fostering a sense of community in every cup we serve.

Our Locations

Plaza Merdeka

Located in the vibrant heart of Kuching, our Plaza Merdeka outlet offers a perfect blend of urban convenience and cozy ambiance. Whether you're grabbing a quick coffee before work or relaxing with friends, this location is ideal for all your caffeine needs.

One Jaya

Situated in the bustling One Jaya shopping mall, this outlet is perfect for a leisurely coffee break during your shopping spree. Enjoy our full menu in a modern, comfortable setting with plenty of seating options.
